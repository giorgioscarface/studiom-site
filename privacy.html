Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Pre-release draft. This policy must be reviewed and updated against the implemented product and the operator's confirmed contact details before it is presented as a final public policy or submitted for review.

1. About StudioM

StudioM is a Windows desktop content studio in development. This page describes the intended approach to privacy during testing and will be revised to reflect the actual services provided.

2. Information involved in testing

When an eligible test user elects to connect TikTok, TikTok may provide an account identifier and basic profile data, such as a display name and avatar, within the scopes authorized by that user. Authentication may also involve access and refresh tokens. StudioM should request only the permissions needed for the user-selected functionality.

3. Media and project data

The intended product is local-first. Imported media and editable project files are intended to remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses an online feature. The implemented behavior, external processing providers and retention rules must be confirmed before public release.

4. Use and disclosure

Account data is intended to support authorized account connection and user-requested functions, not unrelated advertising or sale of personal information. Third-party platforms, including TikTok, apply their own policies when users authenticate with them.

5. Security and retention

Authentication credentials and tokens must be protected, access must be restricted, and disconnect and token-revocation flows must be implemented. Specific encryption, retention, deletion and hosting arrangements are pending technical verification; no unverified protection or deletion guarantee is made here.

6. User choices and requests

Users should be able to decline optional connections. Test participants may revoke StudioM's TikTok authorization from TikTok's account settings. Requests concerning data held by StudioM should be directed to the operator using the contact method provided after verification.

7. Contact

Contact details pending operator confirmation. This page is not ready for external app review until a functioning privacy contact method and accurate operational details have been supplied.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as StudioM's features and processing practices are implemented. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date.
