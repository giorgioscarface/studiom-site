StudioM Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

StudioM is a local-first Windows media creation application in private development. This policy explains how StudioM's preview features are intended to handle information when a user chooses to connect an external creator-platform account. The features available in any build may differ; no connection or publishing feature should be assumed available until it works in that build.

1. Information accessed with permission

When you choose to authorize a Google or YouTube connection, the requested permissions may allow StudioM to access your YouTube channel identifier, channel name, profile details and other information within the scopes shown on Google's consent screen. OAuth authorization can provide access and refresh tokens so the authorized connection can be maintained. StudioM does not ask for or receive your Google password.

Other optional integrations, including TikTok and Instagram, may access basic account identity and authorization tokens only after their respective login flows are available and you authorize them. Their capabilities and permissions vary by provider and development stage.

2. Why information is used

Authorized account information is used to identify the connected account in StudioM, support features you select, maintain or end a connection, and make the connection's status understandable to you. Any optional channel-content features must be limited to the permissions the user actually grants. A login alone does not authorize StudioM to publish, comment, modify content, or act on your behalf without an additional user-approved workflow.

StudioM may also retrieve publicly available YouTube information for optional trends and discovery features using YouTube API Services. Those public-information features are distinct from personal OAuth authorization.

3. Local projects and optional online services

StudioM is designed to keep imported media, project files and edits on the user's computer for core local workflows. Some separately selected online features or account connections may communicate with Google, YouTube or other service providers. Any future optional cloud-AI processing must be disclosed to the user before being enabled; its availability is not assumed here.

4. Storage, security and retention

StudioM's account-connection design uses local, operating-system-protected credential storage where that functionality is implemented. An approved HTTPS broker may be needed for some providers; a broker is not the same as this static informational website. No claim is made that all proposed integrations are currently deployed, that every token is stored in one place, or that security can be guaranteed.

Account identifiers, connection metadata and authorization credentials are retained only as needed for the enabled connection and applicable user-requested features, subject to provider rules. When a connection is removed, StudioM should remove the related local authorization data. Revoking access at the provider prevents future authorized access but does not itself erase content already stored on that provider's platform.

5. Disclosure and external policies

StudioM does not sell personal account information or use connected-account data for unrelated advertising. Information is shared with a connected platform only as needed for authorized sign-in, token management or features you choose to use. The platform's own policies and terms also apply.

StudioM uses or plans to use YouTube API Services. Please also review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

6. Your choices, revocation and deletion

You may decline an optional platform connection. For Google account connections, you can review or revoke an app's access at Google Account third-party connections. You may also use StudioM's disconnect controls when they are available in your build.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of account-related data held by StudioM through the support contact described below. For authorized YouTube API data, deletion requests will be handled as soon as possible and within seven calendar days where required by YouTube's policies. Deleting StudioM-held data does not delete data retained by YouTube or Google; manage that data directly with the relevant service.

7. Contact

For private account-data requests, use the support email displayed on StudioM's Google OAuth consent screen. For general, non-sensitive questions, open an issue in the StudioM website repository. GitHub issues are public and may require a GitHub account; do not post email addresses, passwords, tokens or other sensitive information there.

8. Policy updates

This page may be updated as features become operational or provider requirements change. Significant changes to account-data processing should be communicated before affected users authorize the new use.
