StudioM

StudioM is an AI-first, local-first Windows desktop content studio in development. It is designed to help a creator plan, organize, edit, review and export videos and audio, with AI-assisted and manual workflows sharing an editable project.

Optional account connections

StudioM is developing optional connections for YouTube and other creator platforms such as TikTok and Instagram. Google/YouTube authorization, where enabled, is requested through Google's consent flow for specific, user-selected features. Connecting an account is separate from authorizing uploads, posts or other account actions.

These integrations remain in development and may be unavailable or limited in a particular preview build. StudioM is intended for a small private testing audience; this site does not offer public downloads.

Privacy and support

Please read the Privacy Policy before connecting an account and the Terms of Service before using an applicable preview.

For general support, contact the StudioM project through GitHub Issues. Issues are public; never include passwords, OAuth tokens or other personal account information.
